# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By taking part you agree to uphold it.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and experience level, accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass, insult or discriminate against anyone. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers at Zhuwa Africa privately (for example through a private GitHub security advisory or by contacting the repository owners). Reports are handled confidentially.
